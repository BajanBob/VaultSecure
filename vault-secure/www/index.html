  How Vault Secure protects the file
  
    
    
  • Encryption: AES-256-GCM, the cipher used by banks and governments, with a key made fresh for every vault by your device’s secure random generator. GCM also detects any tampering.
    • 
    
  • Hybrid key: with a passphrase, the QR key and the passphrase (stretched with 600,000 rounds of PBKDF2) are mixed through HKDF-SHA-256. Both are needed.
    • 
    
  • Quantum-resistant: quantum computers break public-key systems like RSA. Vault Secure uses no public keys; a 256-bit AES key keeps about 128-bit strength even against a quantum attacker, which is considered safe.
    • 
    
  • Tokens: the encrypted file is cut into numbered tokens, each sealed with its own HMAC-SHA-256 tag, then dealt out across the carriers.
    • 
    
  • Smart placement: in pictures and songs the tokens go into the busiest pixels and loudest samples first, where tiny changes are least noticeable, in an order only the key can reproduce. Videos and documents carry them in an extra data block that players and readers ignore.
    • 
    
  • Locked QR: the QR code opens with a notice that it is locked for a specific period, so a normal scanner shows that message instead of a usable link. Only Vault Secure reads the key inside it. Add a passphrase and the key text alone is useless.
    • 
    
  • Long-term check-ins: vaults kept 2 to 17 years get a check-in every 6 months from year 2. Vault Secure beeps and asks you to renew, turn on automatic renewal, or terminate. Unanswered check-ins follow the rule you chose after 30 days. Check-ins are kept on the device that made the vault; add the calendar reminders so your phone beeps even when Vault Secure is closed.
    • 
    
  • Expiry: the expiry time is sealed into the encryption, so it cannot be edited. Vault Secure refuses to open a vault after it expires and the timer shows how long is left. Anyone who keeps the QR key, the carriers and a modified program could still try after expiry; for hard deletion, destroy the QR code when the timer ends.
    • 
    
  • Nothing leaves your device. All of this runs in your browser. No file or key is uploaded anywhere.
